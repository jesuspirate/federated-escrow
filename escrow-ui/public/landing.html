Built different

Every design decision serves one goal: eliminate trust.

🔐
Shamir Secret Sharing
E-cash notes split into 2-of-3 shares. Server can never access funds unilaterally. Cryptographically enforced.
⚡
Fedimint E-Cash
Blinded tokens, instant settlement, zero routing fees. Privacy by default, not by policy.
🔑
Nostr Authentication
NIP-98 signed sessions. One key, one identity. No passwords, no emails, no accounts to manage.
🌍
M-Pesa via ChapSmart
Bitcoin to mobile money. Send TZS, buy airtime, or purchase sats. Real-world rails for East Africa.
